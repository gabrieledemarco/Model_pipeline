Interpretation Guide

Fan chart: shaded bands show 5-95%, 10-90%, 25-75% equity ranges across all 10,000 simulations. The white dashed line is the actual realised equity path (original trade ordering). If it's near the median (p50), luck played little role.

P(+Return): fraction of simulations ending in profit. If >50%, the strategy has positive expectancy regardless of trade ordering.

P(DD>30%): probability of hitting a 30% drawdown at some point. Low values mean the strategy is resilient even under adverse orderings.

Scatter (Return vs Max-DD): each dot is one simulation, coloured by Calmar (green = high, red = low). The star ★ marks the actual OOS outcome. A star in the dense green region means the result is typical; an outlier means luck (good or bad) contributed significantly.
