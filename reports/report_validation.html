Note on sample size: With only 23 WF windows (tests a, b) the t-test has low power. A p-value of ~0.17 with 23 samples does not prove the strategy is random — it means we cannot reject H0 with 95% confidence at this sample size. The per-trade t-test (n=985) is more powerful. The Monte Carlo results (P(Profit)=82.2%) are more informative than the t-test for this regime.
