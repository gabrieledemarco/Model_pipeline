Strategy Logic

1 — Range Detection

The market is classified as ranging when ADX < 25 AND the price band (rolling 24-bar high − low) / low < 10%. Range boundaries are computed using shifted highs/lows (no look-ahead).

2 — Volume Bias (Accumulation vs Distribution)

Accumulation: OBV is above its 21-bar EMA within a ranging market — smart money is absorbing supply without moving price. Distribution: OBV is below its 21-bar EMA — smart money is quietly unloading.

3 — Manipulation Events

Spring (long setup): The current bar's low pierces below the range boundary AND closes back inside, on volume ≥ 1.3× 20-bar average, within an accumulation context. This is the classic Wyckoff spring — a false breakdown designed to shake out weak longs before the mark-up phase begins.

Upthrust (short setup): The current bar's high pierces above the range boundary AND closes back inside, on elevated volume, within a distribution context. The mirror image of the spring — a false breakout designed to attract late longs before mark-down.

4 — Entry Timing

Signal fires on the bar after the spring/upthrust event (next-bar open execution). No look-ahead: the signal bar's open price is used for entry.

5 — SL/TP Walk-Forward Optimisation

For each 6-month IS window, a grid search over SL ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5} × TP1 ∈ {1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0} ATR multiples selects the combo with the highest Calmar ratio (minimum 5 IS trades). The TP2 = 2×TP1, TP3 = 3×TP1 scale automatically. The selected params are applied to the OOS window.

6 — Composite Score

Score = (penetration depth / ATR) × vol_ratio × 2. Positive for longs (spring), negative for shorts (upthrust). Used for analytics; all valid events are traded (no score threshold).
