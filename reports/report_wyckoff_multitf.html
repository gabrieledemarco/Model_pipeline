Methodology Notes

Range detection: rolling max(high) and min(low) over the last n_range bars (shifted 1 bar to prevent look-ahead), classified as ranging when ADX < adx_max AND bandwidth < range_width_max.

Accumulation / Distribution bias: OBV above/below its 21-bar EMA within a ranging period — smart money absorbing vs unloading supply.

Spring (long): bar pierces below range support AND closes back inside, on vol_ratio ≥ 1.3, in accumulation context. Entry on next bar open.

Upthrust (short): bar pierces above range resistance AND closes back inside, on elevated volume, in distribution context. Entry on next bar open.

IS optimisation: for each 6-month window, 20 (SL, TP1) combos are evaluated on IS trades. Best combo (highest Calmar or return if Calmar ≤ 0) applied to 2m OOS window.
